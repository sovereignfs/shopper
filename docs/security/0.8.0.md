# Security review: 0.8.0 (workstream 0002)

**Date:** 2026-11-27 · **Scope:** what task 12.8 asks of workstream 0002 (RFC 0005 to 0007): the export route and the records it serves, the sharing
of loyalty cards and their keys, and Inventory's exclusion from pulls, read on `develop` at version 0.8.8. **Written late:** cards were published in
0.8.0 to 0.8.7 without it. **By:** Claude Code, at the owner's request; not an independent review, and **not an audit**: the suite's encryption it
builds on is not yet audited either (`sovereign`'s RFC 0005).

## What was checked

The code was read and the tests below run on a SQLite file; once the fixes were in, the unit tests passed on sqld and PostgreSQL too. Thirteen guards
were also taken out one at a time, and a test seen to fail for each: Inventory's rows and tombstones in a pull; a card's rows, keys and links in a
pull; the Admin condition on writing a card and on making a link to it; the condition on writing a copy of its keys, and that copy's deletion at
removal; others' cards and Inventory in an export; and the export's password and its recent sign-in.

## Findings

1. **A person on a card only was handed the group's starter categories in their export** (core's, and the readable file): their order, icons and which
   the owner had hidden. No name a person wrote was in them, and sync never handed them these. Published in 0.8.0 to 0.8.7. **Fixed in 0.8.8**
   (`src/portability/records.ts`; its test in `records.test.ts` seen to fail without the fix).
2. **An expired link's copy of the keys stays on the server** until another link is made for the same card (for a list's link, any list of the same
   group): no job clears it. The link lets nobody join, and the copy opens only with the secret in the link, so it takes both that and the database.
   The 0.2.0 review says the copy goes on accept, revoke or expiry; on expiry that is not yet so. Reproduced. _Suggested:_ a job that empties the
   copies of expired links. **Open, for the owner.**
3. **Removing someone from a card leaves the other waiting links good.** A removed person who holds one of them, not yet used, joins again with it.
   Only the links the removed person made are revoked, and on a card only its Admin makes links. Reproduced. A list's removal revokes the same way
   (read, not reproduced). _Suggested:_ the confirmation says how many links are waiting, or removing revokes them. **Open, for the owner.**
4. **Removing someone from a card does not change its keys** (RFC 0007, settled 8; `DESIGN.md` §13.6): someone who kept them and also got a copy of
   the database could read a later change to the card. `docs/sharing.md` says so. The confirmation in the sharing dialog did not, though RFC 0007 says
   it does. **The confirmation is fixed in 0.8.8:** it says the person loses the card when their devices next sync, that what they saw or noted stays
   theirs, and that the card’s key is not changed yet (`e2e/card-share.spec.ts`, seen to fail before). **The keys stay unchanged until 16.8: open, for
   the owner to accept.**
5. **`docs/encryption.md` said nothing of loyalty cards or Inventory:** not that they are encrypted and under which keys, nor that the server sees who
   is on each card, which lists a card is linked to, and how many there are, nor that removal keeps a card's keys. **Fixed:** it says each of these.
6. **Settings › Data did not say that the downloaded file holds loyalty cards** and Inventory: it named lists, items, products, categories and people.
   The file has every card's number and code in plain text; the page did say it is not encrypted. **Fixed in 0.8.8:** it names them, the cards with
   their numbers and codes (`e2e/export.spec.ts`, seen to fail before).

## Noted

- **Inventory is given to whoever holds a membership of the group itself,** which only the group's own account has: a new group makes that one, joining
  a list makes none, and `adoptListMembers` ends any older one at every start (`src/lists/scope.test.ts`). The pull and the export rest on this.

## Not covered

A real phone and a real camera; the browser tests on sqld or PostgreSQL; a hosted account, and core's key release end to end; fuzzing and load; the
code of the `@sovereignfs/*` packages; an independent review. The sign-in, sign-up and reset forms are the design system's and outside this scope:
this review does not clear them. 12.8's acceptance, a hosted and a standalone account each opening Home, lists, Inventory and a card, was not run.
